# Ben's avatar images

Save each image in this folder with the file name shown. PNG or JPG, square, at least 1024 x 1024.
A plain white or light grey background is fine; the background is cut out afterwards.

## 1. The main image (do this first)

> A 3D rendered cartoon character in a high-quality animated-film style: a cute, small chibi boy who is a
> software developer. Big head, short body, full body standing, facing the viewer, slight smile. Round
> dark-framed glasses, big friendly green eyes, tousled chestnut-brown hair, light skin. Dark green hoodie with
> white drawstrings and a small green "</>" code symbol printed on the chest, black jogger trousers, black
> sneakers with white soles. Over-ear black headphones resting around his neck. Soft studio lighting, gentle
> shadow under the feet, plain white background, no text, no logo, centred, whole body visible with space
> around it. Square image.

Save as **idle.png**. Regenerate until you like his face; everything else is made from this one.

## 2. The poses

In the same chat, upload **idle.png** (or keep going after it) and ask for each pose with:

> Using the exact same character from this image (same face, glasses, hair, hoodie, trousers, shoes,
> headphones, same 3D style, lighting, camera distance and plain white background, full body, square), show him
> **[pose]**.

| File | [pose] |
|---|---|
| wave.png | waving hello cheerfully with his right hand raised, big smile |
| listen.png | listening carefully, one hand cupped behind his ear, headphones on his ears, attentive look |
| think.png | typing on a small open laptop held in front of him, looking down at the screen, focused |
| talk.png | talking and explaining something, one hand open mid-gesture, mouth open as if speaking |
| quiet.png | holding one finger to his lips to say "shh", playful look |
| sleep.png | dozing while standing, eyes closed, head tilted, a sleepy smile |

Optional extras: **happy.png** (thumbs up, for "done"), **oops.png** (scratching his head, for errors).

## Tips

- If the face drifts in a pose, say "keep his face exactly as in the first image" and regenerate.
- Keep the camera the same: full body, centred, same size in the frame.
- No text or watermarks in the picture.
